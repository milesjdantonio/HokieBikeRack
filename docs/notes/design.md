# Design principles

Two surfaces, two jobs:

| Surface | Who | Look |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin terminal** (`/`) | staff | Deliberately plain: one black page that reads like a terminal. Monospace, everything stacked, `[bracket]` buttons. Function over looks. |
| **Customer app** (`/app`) | riders | Crafted with the same principles and effort as Miles's **TerraSim**, **SchmollCrossword** and **Rythm Architect** (summarised below). Also opens from the terminal in a phone frame. |

Third reference: **Rythm Architect** (local dev app, `Downloads/ProjectFrontEnd`, port 5173).

Miles's feedback on the first version: *it feels very AI-generated, where TerraSim is built to
avoid all of that.* The fix is the anti-template rules those projects use (Impeccable's craft
floor and taste-skill's house rules), listed below. They are the main checklist for `/app`.

## From Rythm Architect
- **An own world, one lit centrepiece.** The right half is one living Rive scene (orbit over four lanes) that answers what you do; type is thin and wide; the background moves with the music but stays cheap.
- **Every Rive slot has a non-Rive fallback**; Rive files are generated (`npm run rive`) and checked in the real runtime (`rive:check`) with screenshots of every state.
- **Panels have solid backing**, not `backdrop-filter`.
- **Motion respects reduce-motion** (Motion's `reducedMotion="user"`).
- **Checks before finishing:** type check, unit, e2e, build, and a recorded video of the flow.

## Anti-template rules (Impeccable craft floor + taste-skill house rules)
Banned outright: eyebrow/kicker labels above headings · tiny uppercase letter-spaced labels ·
gradients (backgrounds, buttons, text) · shadows or glows on things at rest (only on what floats:
menus, dialogs) · glass/backdrop blur · coloured side-stripe borders · emoji or Unicode standing in
for icons (draw SVG icons, one stroke weight) · rows of identical icon cards · the hero-metric
template (big number + small label tiles) · index numbers (01/02) · styled accent word in a heading ·
more than two typefaces · a modal for something that doesn't need one.
Do instead: one ground, one ink, one accent (on the action) · surfaces separated by a small step in
tone, not lines · full-ink running text · two or three type sizes doing the work · flat buttons,
corner style decided once · theme the browser surfaces (selection, caret, scrollbars, focus ring,
tabular numerals) · every state (hover, disabled, loading, error, empty) designed · headings two
lines max · copy in the product's own words, controls named by their action.
The admin terminal keeps monospace because Miles asked for a terminal (a brief can earn a default).

## From TerraSim
- **One dark ground, lit things on it.** A single tinted near-black field, solid dark panels, no section bands, no "blueprint costume". (TerraSim: "Solid dark panels with amber actions".)
- **One accent, used for action.** The warm accent marks the primary action and live selection, at most once per screen. Here: Burnt Orange `#e5751f`. Maroon is identity (logo, card), not decoration.
- **Light type on dark.** Light/regular weights glow; heavy bold looks cheap on black. A light display face for headings, a practical grotesk for everything else, **tabular figures** for numbers.
- **Tokens first.** Every colour, font, radius, shadow and easing is a CSS variable in one tokens file.
- **Buttons: one shape, flat fills,** sink a little when pressed.
- **Motion has a reason.** Pulsing only for live state (a rack's heartbeat), never decoration. No marquees, no side-stripe accent borders.
- **Rive from code.** Animations are generated by scripts (`tools/rive/art/`), so they're reviewable in Git. Stable state-machine/input names. **Every animated property has a pose in every state**, so jumping between states never leaves a part stuck mid-animation. A fallback shows if Rive fails.
- **Finished = reviewed and documented.**

## From SchmollCrossword
- **Copy like a big product site** (Miles's rule): short Title Case headings and buttons; quiet Title Case kickers, **not spaced capitals**; **no filler** taglines; no emoji or arrows on buttons; no exclamation marks; no "(parenthetical)" asides; no dot-separated feature lists. Keep names, numbers, status and warnings.
- **Effort is the point.** Animated scenes, a moment for the main success (here: the unlock), an intro on first visit only, sound feedback, phone layouts. Things should feel made, not assembled.
- **Calm shared motion:** a 1–2 px lift on hover, a gentle press, no tilts. Reduce-motion turns the extras off.
- **Self-hosted fonts** (no requests to Google; privacy), SIL OFL, credited in NOTICE.md.
- **In-page dialogs,** never `alert()`/`confirm()`.
- **Robust effects:** an animation error must never kill the loop or the page.
- **Sweep for bugs** on every page and phone width before calling it done.

## Customer app specifics
- Ground `#0e0a0c` (maroon-tinted black), panels `#17111a`→`#211821`, ink `#f4eef0`, muted `#bdb0b6`.
- Fonts: **Fraunces** (light, display) + **Hanken Grotesk** (text). Both OFL, in `frontend/app/fonts/`.
- Rive scenes: `rack.riv` (the lit centrepiece: the rack, a bike, the lock module and reader; idle / granted / denied / alarm / offline), `logo.riv` (the mark draws itself on first visit), `pulse.riv` for live status only.
- Sound: tiny synthesized chirps that mirror the rack's buzzer (granted = one short beep, denied = two low beeps), toggle in Settings.
- No fake data: no bike-occupancy numbers (there's no bike sensor). Sample-only parts are labelled.

## Admin terminal specifics
- Black `#000`, text `#c9d1d9`, dim `#6e7681`, green `#3fb950` OK, amber `#d29922` warning, red `#f85149` alarm, cyan `#58a6ff` links/buttons.
- System monospace (`ui-monospace`, Cascadia Mono, Consolas).
- Sections are "commands" (`$ racks`, `$ events --tail 50`) with plain tables and `█` bars. No animations beyond a blinking cursor.
