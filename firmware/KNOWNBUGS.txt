HokieBikeRack firmware - known bugs and limitations
==================================================

1. Event upload can freeze the main loop for up to 8 seconds
   Where:  src/backend_sync.cpp, syncEvents()
   What:   Every 15 seconds, queued events are uploaded with http.POST().
           That call waits for the server to answer, with an 8-second timeout
           (http.setTimeout(8000)). While it waits, loop() is stuck, so card
           taps, tamper checks, and the lock's auto re-lock timer are not handled.
   When:   Only when Wi-Fi is connected and there are events to send, and the
           backend is slow or unreachable. A fast, healthy server answers quickly.
   Impact: A card tap may be ignored, a shake may be missed, or the lock may stay
           open a few seconds longer than UNLOCK_DURATION_MS.
   Possible fixes (later):
     - Lower the timeout (e.g. 2-3 seconds) as a quick improvement.
     - Run uploads in a separate FreeRTOS task so loop() never waits on the network.
   Status: Open. Acceptable for the demo.
